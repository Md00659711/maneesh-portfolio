## The challenge

A medical supplies business needed a store where clinicians can find the right product fast across a large, technical catalogue, and where the marketing team can build landing pages on their own.

## What I built

- **A custom WooCommerce theme** built for speed and clarity on product-heavy pages.
- **ACF flexible content** so non-developers can compose pages from approved blocks.
- **Algolia search** indexed across the full catalogue for instant, typo-tolerant results.
- **Third-party API integrations** connecting the store to external business systems.

## Stack

PHP, WooCommerce, ACF, Algolia, REST APIs, SCSS, with Git version control throughout.
